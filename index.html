Lamplight · The Second Reading

Before you start

Lamplight reads the day's news and sets it beside a passage of scripture. If you have deliberately stopped reading the news, this brings it back — gently, and once a day, but it does bring it back. And it will not tell you what today means; it puts two things beside each other and stops. If neither of those is what you want, this is probably not for you, and it costs nothing to find that out now.

Which Bible should it read from?

Changeable at any time in Settings. It decides only which books a passage may be drawn from.
